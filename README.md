# Sales Performance Dashboard(PowerBi)

## Project Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, focusing on revenue, profit, orders, customers, and average order value (AOV). It combines key performance indicators with interactive-style visualizations to help users understand sales trends and business performance.

## Key Features

- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Sales and profit performance tracking
- Order and customer overview
- Average Order Value (AOV) analysis
- Sales by product category
- Regional sales comparison
- Top 5 product performance
- Top 5 customer analysis

## Key KPIs

- **Sales:** $3.06M
- **Profit:** $654K+
- **Orders:** 2,000
- **Customers:** 100
- **AOV:** $1,529
- **Goal:** 35.55K
- **Goal Variance:** +59.49%

## Sales Trend

The monthly sales trend highlights fluctuations throughout the year, with **December recording the highest monthly sales**, while February and April show comparatively lower performance.

## Product & Category Analysis

The dashboard includes a category-wise sales breakdown for **Electronics, Furniture, and Office Supplies**. It also highlights the **Top 5 Products**, with Cabinet generating the highest sales, followed by Paper, Keyboard, Mouse, and Printer.

## Regional Performance

Sales are compared across four regions:

- **East:** $892K
- **South:** $787K
- **West:** $709K
- **North:** $670K

The East region records the highest sales performance.

## Customer Analysis

The dashboard highlights the **Top 5 Customers** based on sales contribution, helping identify high-value customers and supporting customer-focused business decisions.

## Dashboard Purpose

This dashboard helps businesses monitor sales performance, identify trends, compare regional results, evaluate product performance, and understand customer contributions. It is suitable for **Business Intelligence, Sales Analytics, Data Visualization, and Portfolio Projects**.
